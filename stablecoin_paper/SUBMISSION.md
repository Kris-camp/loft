## Short abstract (for the web form, ~150 words)
The same dollar stablecoin trades at different prices across local currencies, and identical order flow moves those
prices by very different amounts. Arbitrageurs with a per-unit transfer cost and a convex balance-sheet cost leave the
premium untouched inside a band and trade linearly outside it, so the long-run price impact of order flow equals
primitive impact divided by effective arbitrage capacity: volume measures demand, and the premium measures capacity.
Minute data on USDT against the Turkish lira (2024-2026) match these predictions. The premium does not revert inside a
band of about 30 bp and reverts outside it; arbitrage is four to five times faster when Istanbul banks are open, so the
same order flow has nearly five times the long-run price impact at night; small and medium trades push the premium out
of the band while large trades lean against it, most strongly when banks are open; and the stablecoin price carries the
entire correction toward the interbank rate.
